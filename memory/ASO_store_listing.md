## NOTES / TIPS
- Apple Subtitle and Play Short description are the highest-impact ASO fields after the name — keep the strongest keywords there.
- Apple Keywords: no spaces after commas (maximises the 100-char budget); don't repeat words already in the name/subtitle.
- Localise later for key markets (e.g., en-GB spelling "colour" is used above; switch to "color" for en-US locale).
- Screenshots suggestion (captions): 1) "Dress Me — an outfit every morning" 2) "Your whole wardrobe, beautifully catalogued" 3) "Styled for today's weather" 4) "Find your best colours" 5) "Pack the perfect capsule".
- Category: Lifestyle (primary), Shopping or Productivity (secondary).
